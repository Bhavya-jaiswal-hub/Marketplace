# Business Glossary

## Seller
A registered individual or business approved by the Super Admin to sell products.

## Category
A permission area that determines what type of products a seller is allowed to sell.

## Commission
A percentage deducted by the platform from each successful sale.

## Settlement
The weekly transfer of earnings from the platform to the seller after deducting commission.

## Return
The process where a customer sends a delivered product back.

## Refund
Returning the customer's payment after the returned product has been verified.

## Super Admin
The owner of the marketplace who manages sellers, categories, commissions, settlements, and can also act as a seller.
